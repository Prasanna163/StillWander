# Still Wander brand sheet

## Core identity

- **Name:** Still Wander
- **Mod ID:** `stillwander`
- **Tagline:** Stand still. See your world differently.
- **One-line pitch:** An environment-aware cinematic camera that brings your Minecraft world to life whenever you step away.
- **Version:** 1.0.0 for Minecraft 1.21.11
- **Loader:** Fabric

## Short listing description

Turn idle moments into smooth cinematic journeys through your Minecraft world. Still Wander automatically frames the player, nearby entities, caves, interiors, and sweeping landscapes with slow camera movement and varied compositions.

## Full listing description

Still Wander transforms AFK time into an automatic cinematic showcase of your Minecraft world. When you stop playing, the camera begins selecting environment-aware shots around the player. It can move from character and entity details to cave passages, structures, forests, and broad landscape views, adjusting its framing to the available space.

The camera uses smooth pans, varied shot lengths, independent cinematic FOV, and randomized compositions to avoid a repetitive orbit around the player. Press `B` whenever you want to begin or end a sequence immediately, and press `N` to cut to another shot.

Still Wander is client-side, requires Fabric API, and does not record video. Your normal controls remain the exit mechanism, and damage can stop the cinematic when **Exit on damage** is enabled.

## Showcase video structure

1. Open with the player standing still and the tagline on screen.
2. Show the automatic transition into a player composition.
3. Cut through a forest passage, cave corridor, entity follow, and broad landscape shot.
4. Demonstrate `B` for immediate activation and `N` for the next shot.
5. End on a wide sunset composition with the mod name and supported version.

Use Replay Mod or OBS for video capture. Still Wander controls the camera but does not generate an MP4 itself.
