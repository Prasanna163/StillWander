## Description

> **AI transparency:** Still Wander was developed with substantial generative-AI coding and publishing assistance. The branding icon is AI-generated illustrative artwork and is not an in-game screenshot. Actual screenshots and video on this page show the mod's real camera behavior.

Still Wander transforms AFK time into an automatic cinematic showcase of your Minecraft world. When you stop playing, the camera selects environment-aware shots around the player, moving between character and entity details, cave passages, structures, forests, and broad landscape views.

The camera uses smooth pans, varied shot lengths, independent cinematic FOV, and randomized compositions to avoid a repetitive orbit around the player. Press **B** whenever you want to begin or end a sequence immediately, and press **N** to cut to another shot.

### Requirements

- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11
- Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer
- Fabric API
- Java 21 or newer

Still Wander is client-side and does not need to be installed on a multiplayer server. Normal player input exits the cinematic, and taking damage exits it when **Exit on damage** is enabled.

### Controls

- **B:** start or stop the cinematic
- **N:** cut to the next shot
- **F7:** open settings
- **F8:** toggle camera-path capture
- **F9:** toggle the diagnostic overlay
